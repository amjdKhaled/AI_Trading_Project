Change the architecture completely.

I do NOT want Generate Engine to generate trade signals anymore.

New architecture:

1. Candle closes (5m or 15m).

2. Generate Engine becomes a Market Analysis Engine only.

Its job is NOT to produce LONG or SHORT.

Its only job is to collect and calculate:

* OHLCV
* RSI
* EMA 20/50/200
* MACD
* ATR
* VWAP
* Relative Volume
* Market Structure
* Liquidity Sweeps
* Order Blocks
* Fair Value Gaps
* Support and Resistance
* Session Analysis
* Trend Direction
* Multi-timeframe Alignment
* Candlestick Patterns
* News Sentiment
* Historical Trade Statistics

3. Generate Engine must output a complete structured market context object.

Example:

{
marketStructure: {},
indicators: {},
liquidity: {},
orderBlocks: {},
fairValueGaps: {},
supportResistance: {},
news: {},
historicalStats: {}
}

4. Send the FULL market context to Ollama.

5. Ollama becomes the Trade Intelligence Engine.

Ollama is responsible for:

* Reading all market context.
* Understanding market conditions.
* Comparing with previous successful trades.
* Comparing with previous failed trades.
* Understanding trend quality.
* Evaluating risk.
* Evaluating reward.
* Detecting traps and fake breakouts.
* Generating the trade idea itself.

6. Ollama must return:

{
decision: LONG | SHORT | WAIT,
confidence: number,
entry: number,
stopLoss: number,
takeProfit: number,
riskReward: number,
reasoning: string,
strengths: [],
weaknesses: [],
marketBias: BULLISH | BEARISH | NEUTRAL
}

7. Final Decision Logic

confidence >= 80
→ APPROVED

confidence 65-79
→ WAIT

confidence < 65
→ REJECTED

8. Chart Markers

Show only:

* APPROVED LONG
* APPROVED SHORT
* REJECTED SETUP

Do NOT display WAIT markers.

9. Marker Details

When clicking a marker display:

* Entry
* Stop Loss
* Take Profit
* Confidence
* Risk Reward
* AI reasoning
* Technical context
* Market structure analysis
* Liquidity analysis
* News sentiment
* Historical trade similarity

10. Trade Learning

After trade closes:

Store in database:

* Full market context
* AI reasoning
* Entry
* Stop Loss
* Take Profit
* Result
* Win/Loss
* Profit
* Confidence

Future Ollama decisions must use this historical database as memory.

11. Database Only

Move all:

* AI memory
* Trade history
* Reflections
* Decisions
* Market snapshots

to the database.

No JSON file storage.

12. Goal

Generate Engine should become a Market Analysis Engine.

Ollama should become the actual Trade Intelligence Engine that generates LONG, SHORT, or WAIT decisions.

The final trade idea must come primarily from Ollama, using the market context generated by the analysis engine.

13. Performance

All calculations must remain deterministic and fast in code.

Ollama should focus on reasoning and decision making, not recalculating indicators.

This keeps indicator calculations accurate while making AI the primary trade decision maker.
