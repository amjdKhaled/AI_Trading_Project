The chart structure and lifecycle management are improving significantly.

However, I noticed that many trades are still low quality and losing frequently.

At this stage, I do NOT want machine learning or AI self-learning yet.

Right now I want to improve the RULE-BASED TECHNICAL ENGINE itself first.

====================================================
CURRENT PRIORITY
================

Focus on improving:

* technical analysis quality
* signal logic
* setup filtering
* market context understanding
* trade selection quality

before introducing AI learning systems.

====================================================
GOAL
====

I want:

* fewer trades
* much higher quality trades
* better win rate
* more professional market reading

The engine should behave like:

* a disciplined professional trader
* not a high-frequency signal spammer

====================================================
IMPROVE TECHNICAL ANALYSIS LOGIC
================================

Improve filtering for:

* weak breakouts
* late entries
* overextended moves
* low-volume setups
* choppy market conditions
* fake momentum
* weak reversals
* bad RR setups
* exhausted trends

====================================================
IMPROVE TREND QUALITY DETECTION
===============================

Avoid entering:

* after major exhaustion candles
* after vertical moves
* directly into resistance/support
* during low-quality consolidation
* during sideways noise

====================================================
IMPROVE MARKET STRUCTURE UNDERSTANDING
======================================

Prioritize:

* clean BOS
* strong trend continuation
* healthy pullbacks
* strong reclaim setups
* clean momentum expansion

Avoid:

* messy structure
* unclear direction
* weak continuation

====================================================
BETTER ENTRY TIMING
===================

Improve entry precision.

I want entries closer to:

* pullbacks
* confirmations
* retests
* momentum continuation

NOT random mid-move entries.

====================================================
SMARTER TRADE FILTERING
=======================

The engine should reject more trades automatically.

No forced trades.
No mediocre setups.
No weak confluence.

Only high-conviction setups should appear.

====================================================
IMPORTANT
=========

Do NOT add AI learning yet.

First:

* strengthen the technical engine
* improve win rate
* improve setup quality
* improve signal discipline

Later we can build AI learning on top of a strong foundation.
