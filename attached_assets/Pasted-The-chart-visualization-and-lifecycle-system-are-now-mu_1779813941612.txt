The chart visualization and lifecycle system are now much more professional.

However, the win rate is still far too low for the level of filtering currently applied.

This means the main remaining weakness is no longer visualization or trade flow.

The weakness is now the TECHNICAL ANALYSIS DECISION ENGINE itself.

====================================================
PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
=================

I want a major improvement in:

* market understanding
* setup quality
* trade timing
* technical decision accuracy

The engine must stop behaving like:

* a reactive signal generator

and start behaving like:

* a professional discretionary trader
* a smart institutional analyst

====================================================
CURRENT PROBLEM
===============

The engine still enters too many trades that are technically valid,
but contextually weak.

Many losing trades come from:

* entering too late
* entering inside weak structure
* entering during exhausted momentum
* entering during unstable consolidation
* entering before confirmation is fully complete

====================================================
I WANT MUCH SMARTER MARKET READING
==================================

The engine should deeply understand:

* trend quality
* momentum quality
* structure cleanliness
* liquidity behavior
* exhaustion
* trap setups
* continuation probability
* reversal probability

====================================================

1. IMPROVE TREND QUALITY ANALYSIS
   ====================================================

Do NOT trade every trend.

Only trade:

* clean trends
* healthy pullbacks
* strong continuation structure
* trends with momentum expansion
* trends with supportive volume

Reject:

* weak trends
* choppy trends
* unstable direction
* noisy movement
* overextended trends

====================================================
2. IMPROVE MARKET STRUCTURE LOGIC
=================================

I want stronger understanding of:

* BOS quality
* liquidity sweeps
* failed breakdowns
* failed breakouts
* trend continuation structure
* higher highs / higher lows quality
* lower highs / lower lows quality

The engine should identify:

* when structure is healthy
* when structure is weak
* when structure is transitioning

====================================================
3. IMPROVE ENTRY TIMING
=======================

Current entries still feel late.

I want entries closer to:

* pullback confirmation
* reclaim candles
* liquidity sweep reversals
* breakout retests
* continuation compression releases

Avoid:

* chasing large candles
* entering after expansion already happened
* entering after momentum exhaustion

====================================================
4. IMPROVE VOLUME ANALYSIS
==========================

Volume should become a major filter.

Strong setups should require:

* real participation
* strong relative volume
* breakout confirmation volume
* continuation support volume

Reject:

* weak-volume breakouts
* weak continuation volume
* fading momentum

====================================================
5. ADD SMART EXHAUSTION DETECTION
=================================

The engine should detect:

* climax candles
* parabolic movement
* momentum exhaustion
* trend fatigue
* overextension
* weakening continuation

and avoid entering near probable exhaustion zones.

====================================================
6. IMPROVE REVERSAL QUALITY
===========================

Do NOT allow weak reversals.

Only allow reversals when:

* structure breaks clearly
* momentum shifts strongly
* liquidity sweep confirms reversal
* volume confirms reversal
* reclaim/rejection confirms direction change

====================================================
7. PRIORITIZE RISK-TO-REWARD QUALITY
====================================

The engine should strongly prefer:

* tight invalidation
* nearby logical stop
* asymmetric reward potential

Reject trades with:

* poor RR
* wide stops
* weak target structure

====================================================
8. TRADE LESS, WIN MORE
=======================

I do NOT want more signals.

I want:

* fewer trades
* significantly higher quality
* stronger confirmations
* much higher win probability

Even if signal count becomes very low,
that is completely acceptable.

====================================================
9. THINK LIKE A PROFESSIONAL TRADER
===================================

The engine should behave like:

* a patient trader
* a selective trader
* a disciplined analyst
* a high-conviction execution system

NOT:

* a reactive indicator bot
* a momentum chaser
* a noisy signal generator

====================================================
10. TARGET RESULT
=================

The goal is NOT visual beauty anymore.

The goal now is:

* dramatically better trade quality
* smarter technical decisions
* cleaner entries
* smaller losses
* stronger winners
* much higher win rate
* professional-grade execution quality
