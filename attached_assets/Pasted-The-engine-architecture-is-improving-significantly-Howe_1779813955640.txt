====================================================
CRITICAL ARCHITECTURE CHANGE
============================

I do NOT want the engine to rely on:

* one strategy
* one setup type
* one market behavior model

I want the system to become:

ADAPTIVE
MULTI-STRATEGY
MARKET-AWARE

The engine should dynamically choose:

* which strategy to use
* when to trade
* when NOT to trade

====================================================

1. MARKET REGIME DETECTION
   ====================================================

The engine must first classify the market before generating any signal.

Examples:

* trending market
* ranging market
* choppy market
* volatility expansion
* volatility compression
* momentum trend
* weak/noisy structure

Different regimes require different trading behavior.

====================================================
2. STRATEGY ADAPTATION
======================

The engine should dynamically switch between multiple strategies.

Example:

TRENDING MARKET:

* breakout continuation
* pullback continuation
* EMA trend entries
* momentum expansion

RANGING MARKET:

* mean reversion
* liquidity sweep reversals
* support/resistance bounces

HIGH VOLATILITY:

* wider targets
* stricter confirmations
* avoid chasing

LOW VOLATILITY:

* avoid breakout traps
* wait for expansion

====================================================
3. MULTI-TIMEFRAME MARKET BIAS
==============================

I want real multi-timeframe analysis.

Not just:
"15m bullish = +12 score"

I want:

* higher timeframe defines market bias
* lower timeframe defines entry precision

Example:

* 1H = structure + direction
* 15m = setup context
* 5m = execution timing

The engine should align trades with higher timeframe structure whenever possible.

====================================================
4. PRIORITIZE PRICE ACTION OVER INDICATORS
==========================================

Indicators should become secondary confirmation tools.

Primary analysis should come from:

* price action
* structure
* liquidity
* momentum
* volume
* candle behavior
* breakout quality
* rejection quality

Indicators should support the decision,
NOT dominate the decision.
