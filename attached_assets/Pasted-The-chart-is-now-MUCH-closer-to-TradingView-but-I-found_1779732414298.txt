The chart is now MUCH closer to TradingView, but I found another important issue:

====================================================
TIME BUCKET ALIGNMENT IS WRONG
==============================

The 5m candles are NOT aligned to proper market intervals.

Right now I see timestamps like:
13:43

But on a true 5m chart they MUST align exactly to:
13:40
13:45
13:50
etc.

This means the aggregation engine is currently creating candles using floating timestamps instead of fixed interval boundaries.

====================================================
THIS CAUSES:
============

* candle structure divergence
* slightly different OHLC
* different candle grouping
* volume mismatch
* different momentum structure
* chart drift from TradingView

Even if the raw data is correct, wrong interval alignment changes the entire candle sequence.

====================================================
REQUIRED FIX
============

Implement STRICT timeframe bucketing.

====================================================
5M RULE
=======

Every 5m candle timestamp MUST be aligned exactly to:

00
05
10
15
20
25
30
35
40
45
50
55

Example:
13:43 data belongs inside the:
13:40 candle

NOT a custom 13:43 candle.

====================================================
15M RULE
========

Every 15m candle MUST align exactly to:

00
15
30
45

Example:
13:43 belongs to:
13:30 candle

====================================================
IMPLEMENT PROPER FLOOR ALIGNMENT
================================

Use deterministic interval flooring:

For 5m:
timestamp = floor(timestamp / 300000) * 300000

For 15m:
timestamp = floor(timestamp / 900000) * 900000

====================================================
IMPORTANT
=========

This alignment must happen:
BEFORE candle aggregation.

Not after rendering.

====================================================
AFTER FIXING
============

Verify:

* candle timestamps match TradingView
* candle boundaries match TradingView
* volume grouping matches TradingView
* OHLC structure becomes closer to TradingView
* no floating timestamps remain
* no custom intervals exist
* all bars snap perfectly to official market intervals

FINAL GOAL
==========

I want the chart intervals to behave exactly like:
TradingView / institutional charting systems.
