====================================================
ADVANCED PROFESSIONAL FEATURES
==============================

Implement advanced institutional-style analysis features.

====================================================
MARKET REGIME DETECTION
=======================

Detect current market conditions:

* trending market
* ranging market
* high volatility
* low volatility
* momentum expansion
* choppy/noisy market

The signal engine must adapt behavior based on market regime.

====================================================
MULTI-TIMEFRAME CONFIRMATION
============================

Use:

* 15m timeframe as higher timeframe bias
* 5m timeframe as execution timeframe

Example:

* bullish 15m trend
* bullish 5m setup
  = stronger LONG signal

Avoid trading strongly against higher timeframe structure.

====================================================
SESSION AWARENESS
=================

Analyze market sessions:

* market open
* midday/lunch consolidation
* power hour
* after-hours behavior

The engine should understand that market behavior changes during different sessions.

====================================================
RISK MANAGEMENT ENGINE
======================

Implement professional risk filtering.

Reject trades when:

* risk/reward is poor
* volatility is too high
* setup quality is weak
* confirmation count is too low

Use:

* ATR-based stop losses
* dynamic take profit
* minimum RR thresholds

====================================================
SIGNAL QUALITY GRADING
======================

Grade setups:

* A+
* A
* B
* Avoid

Only display high-quality setups prominently.

====================================================
TRADE COOLDOWN SYSTEM
=====================

Prevent overtrading.

Examples:

* cooldown after stop loss
* cooldown after failed breakout
* avoid multiple signals in same consolidation zone

====================================================
BACKTESTING SUPPORT
===================

Build the signal engine so it can later support:

* historical replay
* backtesting
* winrate analysis
* profit factor analysis
* drawdown analysis

The architecture must be scalable for future strategy optimization.
