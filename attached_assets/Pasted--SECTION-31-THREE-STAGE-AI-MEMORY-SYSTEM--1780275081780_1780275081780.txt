====================================================
SECTION 31
THREE-STAGE AI MEMORY SYSTEM
====================================================

This is one of the most important upgrades in the entire project.

Do NOT use AI Memory only after a trade closes.

AI Memory must participate in ALL THREE phases:

1. BEFORE ENTRY
2. DURING TRADE
3. AFTER TRADE

====================================================
PHASE 1
BEFORE ENTRY
====================================================

Before approving any LONG or SHORT trade:

The AI must query memory and ask:

Have we seen this setup before?

How many times?

What was the win rate?

What caused previous failures?

What market regime performed best?

What confidence levels worked best?

What confidence levels failed?

What volume conditions worked best?

What volume conditions failed?

What session performed best?

What session performed worst?

What was the most recent loss?

What lesson was learned from it?

The AI must use these answers BEFORE deciding.

Memory should influence:

LONG approval

SHORT approval

NO TRADE decision

Confidence

Risk Rating

Trade Grade

SL Placement

TP Placement

Position Quality

====================================================
PHASE 2
DURING TRADE
====================================================

Once a trade becomes active:

The AI must continue thinking.

The AI should NOT stop after entry.

Every new candle:

Re-evaluate the trade.

Compare current behavior against:

Winning historical trades

Losing historical trades

Memory lessons

Failure categories

Recent losses

Historical winners

If the trade starts behaving like historical losers:

Reduce confidence.

Generate warnings.

Flag deteriorating trade quality.

Example:

"Warning: Current trade behavior resembles 72% of previous losing trades."

or

"Volume profile now matches historical failed breakout setups."

or

"Price structure resembles prior stop-loss events."

Store these observations.

Why it failed

What memory signals were wrong

Then update:

Lessons

Patterns

Failure Categories

Setup Rankings

Regime Rankings

Symbol Rankings
