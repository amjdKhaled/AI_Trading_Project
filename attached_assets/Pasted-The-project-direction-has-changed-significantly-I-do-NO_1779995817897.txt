The project direction has changed significantly.

I do NOT want the AI to be a post-trade reflection tool anymore.

The current AI Reflection Engine architecture is no longer the primary purpose of the system.

I want to transform the platform into an AI-FIRST Trading System where the AI participates in the signal generation process itself and becomes the final decision maker before any signal appears on the chart.

==================================================
NEW ARCHITECTURE
================

Current architecture:

Market Data
→ Technical Analysis
→ Signal Generation
→ Chart Markers
→ AI Reflection (after trade)

This is NOT what I want.

New architecture:

Market Data
→ Technical Analysis Engine
→ Pattern Detection Engine
→ Candlestick Analysis Engine
→ Market Structure Engine
→ News & Sentiment Engine
→ Economic Events Engine
→ Trade Memory Engine
→ AI Decision Engine (Ollama)
→ BUY / SELL / NO_TRADE
→ Entry / SL / TP
→ Chart Markers
→ Trade Tracking
→ Learning & Memory Update

The AI must be integrated BEFORE signal generation.

==================================================
AI IS THE FINAL DECISION MAKER
==============================

The AI should receive:

* Symbol
* Timeframe
* OHLCV data
* Volume analysis
* EMA20
* EMA50
* VWAP
* RSI
* ATR
* Support zones
* Resistance zones
* Trend analysis
* Momentum analysis
* Volatility analysis
* Market structure
* BOS
* CHOCH
* Liquidity sweeps
* Breakout detection
* Retest detection
* Candlestick patterns
* Technical chart patterns
* News sentiment
* Economic events
* Previous trade memory
* Previous AI lessons

The AI must analyze ALL of these together before making a decision.

==================================================
AI OUTPUT
=========

The AI should NOT simply evaluate trades.

The AI must CREATE trades.

The AI should return:

{
"decision": "BUY | SELL | NO_TRADE",
"confidence": 0-100,
"entry": number,
"stopLoss": number,
"takeProfit": number,
"riskReward": number,
"reasoning": "...",
"marketBias": "BULLISH | BEARISH | NEUTRAL"
}

==================================================
STOP LOSS & TAKE PROFIT
=======================

IMPORTANT:

I do NOT want fixed SL/TP values.

The AI must calculate them.

The AI should determine:

* Entry
* Stop Loss
* Take Profit

based on:

* volatility
* ATR
* support/resistance
* liquidity zones
* market structure
* risk/reward

The AI decides where SL and TP should be placed.

The system should not hardcode SL or TP.

==================================================
NO TRADE IS A VALID DECISION
============================

The AI must be allowed to reject setups.

If confidence is low:

decision = NO_TRADE

I do NOT want forced signals.

I prefer fewer high-quality signals over many low-quality signals.

==================================================

The chart must visually display the AI decision.

Display:

* BUY marker
* Entry marker
* Stop Loss marker
* Take Profit marker

When AI returns SELL:

Display:

* SELL marker
* Entry marker
* Stop Loss marker
* Take Profit marker

These must be rendered directly on the chart.

I want to visually see:

BUY
SL
TP

or

SELL
SL
TP

at the exact locations chosen by the AI.

==================================================
AI MEMORY SYSTEM
================

Every completed trade should be stored.

Store:

* Symbol
* Timeframe
* Indicators
* Pattern
* Entry
* SL
* TP
* Result
* Win/Loss
* Profit
* AI reasoning

After the trade closes:

The AI should analyze:

* Why it won
* Why it lost
* What conditions were present
* What mistakes were made

Store those lessons in memory.

Future decisions should use those lessons.
