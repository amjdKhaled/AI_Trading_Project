The engine became over-filtered and is now missing too many high-quality momentum opportunities, especially on volatile Nasdaq stocks like TSLA and NVDA.

I do NOT want an ultra-defensive institutional engine with very low trade frequency.

I want a balanced high-performance intraday engine that:
- captures strong momentum moves
- keeps reasonable trade frequency
- maintains positive expectancy
- adapts dynamically instead of hard-blocking trades

Current issue:
The combination of:
- score floor 97
- 6 confluence pillars
- hard RSI blocks
- RVOL hard skip
- strict EMA distance
- one-trade rule
has reduced opportunity flow too aggressively.

Main objective now:
Increase opportunity capture WITHOUT returning to low-quality spam signals.

Implement these changes:

1. Replace hard filters with adaptive scoring
- Do NOT hard reject RSI >75 or <25.
- Instead apply dynamic penalties based on trend strength and volatility.
- Strong trend + strong volume should override RSI exhaustion.

2. Dynamic score threshold
- Trending regime:
  allow lower score threshold (88–92)
- Choppy/range regime:
  require higher threshold (95–98)

3. Adaptive confluence requirement
- Strong trend regime:
  allow 4–5 pillars
- Chop/range:
  require 6+

4. Replace strict RVOL hard skip
Current RVOL >=0.80 is too restrictive.
Use:
- soft penalty below 0.8
- hard reject only below 0.55

5. Relax EMA distance logic
Current 1.5% limit blocks valid momentum continuation.
Use ATR-relative distance:
- strong trend: allow wider extension
- range/chop: require tighter pullback

6. Remove excessive anti-momentum behavior
The engine currently punishes strong vertical moves too aggressively.
Do NOT block momentum continuation setups automatically.
Instead:
- distinguish between exhaustion and institutional continuation
- use volume + candle structure + pullback quality

7. Improve trend continuation entries
Focus heavily on:
- pullback continuation
- breakout retest
- liquidity sweep continuation
- higher low / lower high structure

These are the best-performing setups on TSLA/NVDA intraday.

8. Modify one-trade rule
Current one-trade-at-a-time logic is too restrictive.
Allow:
- pyramiding continuation entries
OR
- allow re-entry after partial TP / break-even protection

9. Add market session awareness
Increase aggressiveness during:
- market open
- power hour
Reduce aggressiveness during midday chop.

10. Add confidence scaling
Instead of binary signal/no signal:
- produce confidence tiers:
  A+, A, B
Only top tier gets full size.

11. Build separate symbol profiles
TSLA and NVDA behave differently.
The engine should learn per-symbol behavior:
- volatility profile
- average impulse size
- pullback behavior
- trend persistence

12. Prioritize expectancy over raw win rate
Do NOT optimize only for win rate.
Optimize for:
- expectancy
- profit factor
- net R multiple
- opportunity efficiency

Target:
A balanced aggressive-smart engine with:
- higher opportunity flow
- better trend participation
- controlled drawdown
- scalable intraday execution
without becoming over-filtered.
