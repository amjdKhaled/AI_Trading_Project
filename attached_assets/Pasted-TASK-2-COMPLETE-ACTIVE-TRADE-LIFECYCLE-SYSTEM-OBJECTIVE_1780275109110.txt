TASK #2 – COMPLETE ACTIVE TRADE LIFECYCLE SYSTEM

OBJECTIVE

The project currently generates AI trade signals.

However, a signal is not enough.

I want a complete active trade lifecycle system similar to professional trading platforms.

The AI should not simply create a BUY or SELL marker.

The AI should create, monitor, manage, close, archive, and learn from every trade.

====================================================
CURRENT PROBLEM
===============

The chart shows entries.

The chart shows historical trades.

But active trades do not feel alive.

The user cannot visually track:

Current TP zone

Current SL zone

Current RR

Trade health

Trade status

Trade lifecycle

The trade does not visually behave like a real position.

Reward Zone
