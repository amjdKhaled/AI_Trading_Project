====================================================
HISTORICAL CHART LEARNING
=========================

The AI should not only learn from completed trades.

The AI should also learn from historical market behavior.

For every symbol:

* TSLA
* NVDA
* AAPL
* AMD
* MSFT
* QQQ

Build a historical pattern database.

The AI must continuously analyze:

* Previous rallies
* Previous crashes
* Breakouts
* Failed breakouts
* Trend reversals
* Pullbacks
* Consolidations
* High volume moves
* Low volume moves
* Support and resistance reactions

Store historical examples.

Example:

TSLA
Date: 2025-04-18

Pattern:
Bull Flag

Result:
+12%

Conditions:

* Above VWAP
* Strong volume
* Bullish market

Outcome:
Successful breakout

Store this setup.

====================================================
SIMILARITY ENGINE
=================

Before creating a new AI signal:

Search historical setups.

Questions:

* Has this setup appeared before?
* How many times?
* Win rate?
* Average move?
* Average drawdown?
* Similar market conditions?
* Similar news environment?

Return:

Historical Match Score

Example:

Current setup matches 87% of a previous TSLA breakout pattern.

Historical Result:
Win Rate: 73%
Average Gain: 8.4%
Average Duration: 3.2 days

====================================================
SELF REVIEW
===========

After every trade closes:

Compare:

* What AI expected
* What actually happened

Then compare the trade against historical examples.

Determine:

* Was the historical comparison correct?
* Did the AI ignore a warning sign?
* Was the news stronger than the pattern?
* Was market structure misread?

Store the lesson in memory.

The system should become smarter over time through historical pattern recognition and trade review.
