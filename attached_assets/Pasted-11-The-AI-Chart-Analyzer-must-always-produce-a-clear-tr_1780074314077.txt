11. The AI Chart Analyzer must always produce a clear trading decision.

Current behavior:
The system sometimes returns:

* Neutral
* No Trade

without providing a complete trade plan.

Required behavior:

The AI must always classify the chart into one of the following:

* LONG
* SHORT
* NO_TRADE

and explain exactly why.

12. If the result is LONG:

Return:

* Direction: LONG
* Entry Price
* Stop Loss
* Take Profit 1
* Take Profit 2
* Take Profit 3
* Risk/Reward Ratio
* Confidence %
* Success Probability %

13. If the result is SHORT:

Return:

* Direction: SHORT
* Entry Price
* Stop Loss
* Take Profit 1
* Take Profit 2
* Take Profit 3
* Risk/Reward Ratio
* Confidence %
* Success Probability %

14. If the result is NO_TRADE:

The AI must explain:

* Why no trade exists.
* What condition is missing.
* What breakout level would create a LONG.
* What breakdown level would create a SHORT.
* What confirmation candle is required.
* What volume condition is required.

15. Display the decision prominently at the top of the page.

Examples:

🟢 LONG
Confidence: 84%
Entry: 440.50
SL: 437.80
TP1: 444.00
TP2: 447.50
TP3: 452.00

🔴 SHORT
Confidence: 81%
Entry: 440.50
SL: 443.20
TP1: 436.00
TP2: 432.00
TP3: 428.00

🟡 NO TRADE
Reason:
Price is trapped between support and resistance.
Wait for a breakout above 444.00 for LONG confirmation.
Wait for a breakdown below 437.00 for SHORT confirmation.

16. The AI must determine direction using:

* Chart image
* Candlestick patterns
* Market structure
* Trend analysis
* Volume analysis
* Support & Resistance
* Historical chart memory
* Previous successful and failed setups
* AI Memory lessons
* News sentiment (if available)

17. The final result should resemble a professional hedge fund trade plan, not a generic chart description.

The AI's primary objective is to answer:

"Would I take a LONG, SHORT, or NO_TRADE position right now, and where exactly should I enter, place stop loss, and take profit?"
